💡 Key Insights & Interpretation
📈

Massive Surge in 2026

With 1,322 cases in 2026 (up to Sept), the court saw a +599% increase over all of 2025 (189 cases), suggesting either a backlog clearance drive or a significant rise in filings.

⚖️

Bail Applications Dominate

813 First Bail Applications account for 42.4% of all cases. BA1 (bail) is the single most common case code with 873 occurrences, reflecting significant criminal litigation activity.

📋

Order Coverage at 55.6%

1,067 cases have associated order dates from 10–15 Sept 2026, while 852 cases are still pending orders. This suggests ~44% of matters require judicial attention.

🏛️

State Is Primary Respondent

State of Uttarakhand appears as respondent in 803+ cases, meaning the government is defending the majority of civil/criminal petitions filed before the High Court.

🔢

WPMS & Writ Petitions Rising

Writ petitions (WPMS, WPSS, WPCRL, WPSB, WPMB) combined account for 357+ cases. This signals increasing constitutional and administrative challenges being raised against state authorities.

🌍

Party Name Search is Niche

Only 39 cases were retrieved via the Petitioner/Respondent "Uttrakhand" search — just 2% of total — indicating that party-name searches yield highly targeted, narrow result sets.
